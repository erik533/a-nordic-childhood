Your First Numbers pilot is ready.

Your honest observations matter more than a perfect session. If printing fails or your child wants to stop, that is useful feedback too.

1. Download and print

Choose the paper size used by your printer. In the US, choose US Letter; in most other countries, choose A4. Print at actual size or 100%, in colour if convenient. You will also need a pencil or crayon and eight small objects.

2. Try it in two short sittings.

Follow the grown-up directions, but do not teach toward a “right” performance. Notice where the child pauses, changes strategy, asks for help, or loses interest.
