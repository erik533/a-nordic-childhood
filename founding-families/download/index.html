Your First Numbers pilot

Your First Numbers pilot is ready.

Your honest observations matter more than a perfect session. Stop when interest runs out. Printing trouble is worth reporting too.

1. Download and print

Choose the paper size used by your printer. In the US, choose US Letter. In most other countries, choose A4. Print at 100% or Actual Size. Color is optional.

The file contains 19 numbered pages paired across 10 landscape printer sheets. The guide mentions two short sittings, but one honest activity attempt is enough for this pilot. A second sitting is optional.

You will also need a pencil or crayon and up to eight small safe objects, such as buttons, beans, pebbles, or blocks.

2. Try at least one short activity.

Read the grown-up prompt, notice what the child does, and avoid teaching toward a perfect answer. You do not have to finish the pack.

3. Send feedback by .

Five short prompts ask what you tried, one thing you noticed, and where the pages or directions got in the way. The link remains open for a three-day grace period.
