Plain-language privacy note · First Numbers pilot

Small study, small amount of data.

Last updated: 21 August 2026. This note covers only the First Numbers Founding Families pilot.

Who is responsible

Erik Astrand / A Nordic Childhood is responsible for the pilot information. Questions, access requests, corrections, or withdrawal requests can be sent to erik@erikastrand.com.

What we collect

We collect the participating adult’s first name and email, broad child age band, activity language, broad learning-stage answers, print format, pilot status, download and reminder events, and the grown-up’s feedback.

We do not ask for the child’s name, exact birth date, school, address, photograph, audio, or video. Please do not put those details in free-text answers.

Why we use it

We use the information to select a small, relevant pilot group; deliver the pilot; send up to three practical reminders; understand where the material succeeds or fails; prevent duplicate or abusive submissions; and decide whether the agreed feedback qualifies for the pilot thank-you reward.

The pilot emails are operational messages, not general marketing. Permission for one follow-up question is optional and is not marketing consent.

Where it is handled

The pages are hosted by Vercel, pilot records are stored in Supabase, and operational email is delivered through Resend. These providers process data on our behalf under their own security and data-processing terms.

How long we keep it

We keep identifiable pilot information while the pilot and reward review are active. We plan to delete or anonymise it no later than 90 days after the reward is issued or the pilot is closed, unless a longer period is required to resolve a request or legal obligation.

Your choices

You can ask what we hold, request a correction, or withdraw from the pilot by emailing erik@erikastrand.com. Withdrawal stops future reminders and participation. It may not undo analysis that has already been anonymised.

This pilot is for a grown-up to operate. Please do not allow a child to submit either form independently.
