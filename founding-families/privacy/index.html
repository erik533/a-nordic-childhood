Plain-language privacy note · First Numbers pilot

Small pilot, small amount of data.

Last updated: 21 August 2026. This note covers only the First Numbers family pilot.

Who is responsible

The data controller is Erik Astrand, sole proprietor trading as MalmaMedia, Sweden. A Nordic Childhood is the project name. Questions, withdrawals, and privacy requests can be sent to erik@erikastrand.com.

What the pilot collects

We collect the participating adult’s first name and email, the child’s broad age band, one broad learning-stage answer, pilot status, limited source information, downloads, operational reminders, and the grown-up’s feedback.

Source information is limited to a referrer category and the standard utm_source, utm_medium, and utm_campaign fields when they are present. The pilot does not use advertising pixels, cross-site tracking, email-open tracking, or a marketing cookie.

We do not ask for the child’s name, exact birth date, school, address, photograph, audio, or video. Please do not include those details in free-text answers.

Why we use it

  • To run the pilot: we use the adult’s contact details, application, status, personal deadline, downloads, reminders, feedback status, and reward record to provide the pack and manage the agreed exchange.
  • To select a relevant small group: we use the broad age band and learning-stage choice to keep the pilot focused on the pages being tested.
  • To improve First Numbers: we use grown-up observations and limited funnel events to find instruction, activity, printing, and completion problems.
  • For optional future updates: only after qualifying feedback, the adult may make a separate choice to join the First Numbers updates list. This choice does not affect the pilot reward.

Pilot emails are operational messages. Applying does not add anyone to a marketing list.

How application decisions work

Erik reviews the first five likely-fit or unsure applications. After the first-wave review, clear-fit applications may receive an immediate place, unsure applications are reviewed manually, and clear earlier-stage or later-stage answers receive a not-this-round response. The current group is capped at twenty accepted families.

The decision is about the fit of a narrow early draft. It is not an assessment of the child. You may email Erik if you think an application was misunderstood.

Where information is handled

Vercel hosts the pages and application functions. Supabase stores pilot records. Resend delivers operational email and stores contacts who separately opt in to updates. These providers process information for the purposes described here.

Some processing may take place outside the EU or EEA, including in the United States. Where required, providers use recognized transfer safeguards. You may email us for more information.

How long we keep it

  • Identifiable declined applications are deleted after 30 days.
  • Participant feedback is separated from contact and application details 90 days after the cohort review. The anonymous product observations may then be kept.
  • The adult’s name, email, and reward status are kept until the finished digital book is delivered, then deleted within 90 days.
  • A separate marketing contact remains only while the adult is subscribed. Unsubscribing does not affect the pilot reward.

Your rights and choices

Depending on the circumstances, you may ask for access, correction, deletion, restriction, or a portable copy of your information, and you may object to processing based on legitimate interests. You can withdraw from the pilot at any time.

Deleting the contact record before the finished book is released means we cannot deliver that future reward. It does not undo analysis that has already been made anonymous.

To exercise a right, email erik@erikastrand.com. You may also lodge a complaint with the Swedish Authority for Privacy Protection.

The required fields are needed to assess fit and administer the pilot. You do not have to provide them, but without them we cannot offer a pilot place.

This pilot is for a grown-up to operate. Please do not allow a child to submit either form independently.
