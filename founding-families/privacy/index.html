Plain-language privacy note · First Numbers pilot

Small study, small amount of data.

Last updated: 21 August 2026. This note covers only the First Numbers Founding Families pilot.

Who is responsible

The data controller is Erik Astrand, sole proprietor trading as MalmaMedia, Sweden. A Nordic Childhood is the name used for this project. Questions and privacy requests can be sent to erik@erikastrand.com.

What we collect

We collect the participating adult’s first name and email, broad child age band, activity language, broad learning-stage answers, print format, pilot status, download and reminder events, and the grown-up’s feedback. Our hosting and email providers may also process limited technical information, such as an IP address and browser or device information, to deliver and secure their services.

We do not ask for the child’s name, exact birth date, school, address, photograph, audio, or video. Please do not put those details in free-text answers.

Why we use it and our legal basis

  • To administer the pilot agreement: we use the adult’s contact details, application, pilot status, downloads, operational reminders, feedback status, and reward record to provide the pilot and manage the agreed exchange. The legal basis is taking steps at your request and performing the pilot agreement.
  • To select and improve the pilot: we use the broad learning-stage answers, activity information, limited funnel events, and feedback to select a relevant group, understand where the material succeeds or fails, and prevent duplicate or abusive submissions. The legal basis is our legitimate interest in running a useful, reliable small pilot. We reduce the privacy impact by collecting broad information only and excluding child identifiers and media.
  • For one optional follow-up question: if you tick that option in the feedback form, the legal basis is your consent. You can withdraw it at any time by email.

The pilot emails are operational messages, not general marketing. We will not add you to a marketing list through this pilot.

How application screening works

The application uses fixed rules to compare the answers with the pilot’s stated learning stage, language, practical requirements, and available places. It may immediately offer a place, indicate that the pilot is not the current fit, or place an otherwise suitable applicant on the waiting list. This screening does not produce legal or similarly significant effects. You may email us if you believe the result is wrong and would like it reviewed.

Where it is handled

The pages and application functions are hosted by Vercel, pilot records are stored in Supabase, and operational email is delivered through Resend. These providers act as data processors for the services described here.

Some processing may take place outside the EU/EEA, including in the United States. Where required, the providers use recognised transfer safeguards such as an adequacy framework or the European Commission’s Standard Contractual Clauses. You may email us for more information about the safeguards relevant to your information.

How long we keep it

We keep identifiable pilot information while the pilot and reward review are active. We plan to delete or anonymise it no later than 90 days after the reward is issued or the pilot is closed, unless a longer period is required to resolve a request or legal obligation.

Your rights and choices

Depending on the circumstances, you may ask for access, correction, deletion, restriction, or a portable copy of your information, and you may object to processing based on legitimate interests. You can withdraw optional follow-up consent without affecting earlier lawful processing. You can also withdraw from the pilot; this stops future reminders and participation, but cannot undo analysis that has already been anonymised.

To exercise a right, email erik@erikastrand.com. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with the Swedish Authority for Privacy Protection (IMY).

The required application fields are needed to assess fit and administer the pilot. You do not have to provide them, but without them we cannot offer a pilot place.

This pilot is for a grown-up to operate. Please do not allow a child to submit either form independently.
