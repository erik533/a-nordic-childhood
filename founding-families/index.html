The step after counting aloud

Counting words and understanding quantity are different skills.

A child may count aloud with confidence and still lose track when touching objects, know the shape of 5 without knowing how many it represents, or recount a group when its arrangement changes.

The pilot lets the child see, make, and move quantities so the spoken word, written number, and amount begin to belong together.

Inside the pilot

Three numbers. Three ways of understanding.

The previews below are real pages from the pack. A grown-up reads the short prompt and notices what the child does.

Meet Two

See one and two.

Point to a small quantity and say how you knew, without always beginning a count.

Meet Five

Gather five.

Find five small objects, count each one, and place them with the written number.

Meet Eight

Build eight as five and three.

Arrange eight objects in a frame and see how a larger number can be made from smaller groups.

A narrow first round

Could this fit your child?

Age is only a guide. The learning stage matters more.

Likely to fit

  • Can count aloud to ten, perhaps with occasional help.
  • Is still learning to count objects once each, match written numbers to amounts, or recognize the same amount in a new arrangement.
  • Can join a grown-up for one short printable activity in English.

Probably not this round

  • Is only beginning to use number words and is not yet ready to count a small group of objects.
  • Already matches written numbers to amounts confidently and can make five or eight in different groupings with little help.
  • Cannot use a printed English activity with a grown-up during the next seven days.

Short answers

Questions families may have.

What if my child wants to stop?

Stop. Please tell us where interest ran out. That still counts as a real attempt.

What if printing does not work?

Report the problem. A genuine printing failure counts because printing is part of the product being tested.

Why does the printer show 10 sheets for a 19-page pack?

The 19 numbered pages are paired across 10 landscape printer sheets. Print the file at 100% or Actual Size.

Do you collect information from my child?

No. We collect broad learning-stage information from the grown-up, but no child name, exact birth date, school, photograph, audio, or video.

When will the finished book arrive?

There is no fixed release date yet. Participants who complete the feedback will receive the finished digital book free when it is released.

Will I be added to a mailing list?

No. Pilot emails are operational only. After completed feedback, you may separately choose to receive future First Numbers updates.
