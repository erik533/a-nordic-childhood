## Platform

web

## Users

Parents and caregivers of young children who want gentle, useful early learning materials without turning home into school. They need to choose something that fits the child today, use only what helps, and leave the rest without guilt.

## Product Purpose

A Nordic Childhood: The Learning Collection is a printable home-learning collection for early numbers, letters, meaningful writing, nature, feelings, and reflection. Success means helping a family begin with one relevant page, support the child without forcing completion, and sometimes preserve an early mark or message worth keeping.

## Positioning

The collection begins with meaning and real quantities, offers support that can gently disappear, and gives the child room to make an independent mark. Its Nordic character comes from how it sees childhood, not from decorative styling.

## Operating Context

Families receive PDF files, use bookmarks and page ranges to find a suitable page, and print only what is useful. A younger child may need help choosing and beginning. The collection is a home-learning supplement, not a complete curriculum or a promise of unattended independent play.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- 313 printable PDF pages across eight core books and guides.
- The collection includes number, letter, word, nature, feeling, reflection, practice, and printing guidance.
- Pages can be selected by readiness and interest rather than completed in order.
- Personal household use permits repeat printing for children in that household.
- The current stated price is US $29 once.
- No upper age limit should be presented as a firm boundary.

## Brand Commitments

- Product name: A Nordic Childhood: The Learning Collection.
- Calm, honest, warm, and specific voice. Storytelling may be used, but hard-sell agitation must not.
- Nordic roots should be visible as a meaningful distinction while making clear that the collection is for families everywhere.
- Preserve the approved sales-page copy and the established forest, ochre, cream, berry, sage, and slate palette while visual alternatives are tested.
- Never use em dashes in A Nordic Childhood production materials.

## Evidence on Hand

- Real book covers and page previews are stored in `public/`.
- A real family archive page from Christmas 1985 and a real photograph of the author are stored in `public/`.
- The page names three relevant research sources concerning handwriting, symbol learning, and early mathematics.
- There are no customer testimonials yet. Future work must not invent them.

## Product Principles

1. One useful page can be enough.
2. Meaning comes before repetition.
3. Support should help the child begin, then make room for the child's own mark.
4. Nordic ideas give the collection character, but do not limit who it is for.
5. Show the real pages and state claims honestly.
